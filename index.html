outwitted-upload

outwitted-upload is a small desktop application used by the operator of the YouTube channel “Outwitted”, a history channel with short videos about sieges, battles and the decisions that decided them.

What the application does

  • Uploads finished short videos from the operator’s computer to the operator’s own YouTube channel.
  • Sets title, description, tags, category, language, “made for kids” = no and the disclosure for altered or synthetic content.
  • Uploads each video as private with a scheduled publish time, so YouTube publishes it automatically.
  • Reads the list of already scheduled videos on the channel to find the next free publishing slot.

Who can use it

Only the channel operator, signing in with their own Google account. The application is not offered to the public and never accesses other people’s accounts or channels.

Google user data

The application requests the scopes youtube.upload and youtube.readonly and uses them only for the functions listed above. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
