Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

The Outwitted Upload Tool is a private application that runs only on the channel operator’s own computer. It is used solely to upload and schedule videos to the operator’s own YouTube channel.

Data accessed

With the operator’s consent via Google OAuth, the tool uses the YouTube Data API v3 with these scopes:

  • youtube.upload – to upload videos to the operator’s channel
  • youtube.readonly – to read the channel name and the list of already scheduled videos

Storage and sharing

  • The OAuth access token is stored only locally on the operator’s computer.
  • No data is sent to any server other than Google’s.
  • No data from other users is collected, stored or shared. The tool is not offered to third parties.
  • No analytics, cookies or tracking are used.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. See also the Google Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service.
